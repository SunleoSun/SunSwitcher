SCOWL is derived from many sources, most of which are in the Public Domain. Data from the Corpus of Contemporary American English (COCA) was also used.

The generated English word list uses size 60, American spelling, variant level 1, and disables special categories. SCOWL states that for an official non-Australian speller dictionary the notice above is sufficient; this asset does not use Australian spelling or a list larger than size 80.
